# 10 — Scope: what the prototype demonstrates vs. what a paid agreement would include

The initial discussion was **about AED 500 for basic website work**, possibly more for social branding. This prototype goes well beyond that to show what's possible. It is **not** an offer to deliver all of it for that amount.

## Demonstrated in this prototype (unofficial concept)
- Seven-route website design and frontend build; scent signature and map system; shop hand-off to the existing store; brief builder.
- Sample social kit (26 frames, one reel) and the generator.
- Research, documentation, QA, commerce handoff.

## A sensible first paid phase (suggested, to be agreed)
- Content and image approvals (doc 06); swap in the official logo and approved photography.
- Production deployment on the company's domain; remove `noindex`; basic analytics with a consent decision.
- One round of copy and layout revisions.

## Separate, optional work (each needs its own agreement)
- WooCommerce investigation and fixes with authorised access (doc 07): order visibility, notifications, store cleanup.
- Ongoing social content (new scent posts and reels from the generator); no account management included.
- Arabic version (RTL), commissioned photography, per-volume pricing and add-to-cart deep links.
- Hosting and maintenance terms.

## Not included at any stage unless separately scoped
Custom checkout or payments, a new order database, CRM/ERP, customer accounts, staff dashboards, ad buying or account management.
